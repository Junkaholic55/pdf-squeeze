PDF Squeeze - make PDFs with pictures smaller
=============================================

Setup (once)
------------
1. Copy the whole PDFSqueeze folder somewhere you can write to, for example Documents\PDFSqueeze.
   Keep the "_internal" folder next to PDFSqueeze.exe - the app needs it.
2. Right-click PDFSqueeze.exe > Show more options > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).

Nothing is installed. To remove it, delete the folder.

Use
---
1. Drag a PDF onto PDFSqueeze.exe or its desktop shortcut.
   (Or double-click it and pick a PDF.)
2. Pick a compression level:
     High quality - 200 DPI, for printing and fine detail
     Balanced     - 150 DPI, good for email and archiving
     Smallest     - 96 DPI, for upload limits and on-screen reading
3. Click Compress.

A smaller copy is saved next to the original as "<name>_compressed.pdf".
The original file is never changed.
Text stays selectable and searchable; links, bookmarks and forms are kept.

If it says "Already optimized", the PDF cannot be made meaningfully smaller at that level.

First run
---------
Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not code-signed.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Problems
--------
A log is kept at %LOCALAPPDATA%\PDFSqueeze\log.txt
